Core Privacy Summary

MicrobusinessFlow is an offline-first financial management application. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit your financial bookkeeping, expense records, or business balances to our servers. All your transactions remain securely on your local device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because MicrobusinessFlow operates completely on-device, our servers never receive, process, or retain any of the following:

  • No Financial Records: Your recorded income, expenses, categories, budget limits, and notes are saved exclusively in a local SQLite database (Android Room) on your physical device.
  • No User Account or Registration: You are not required to create an account, log in with an email, or submit any personally identifiable information (PII) to use the app.
  • No Contact or Location Data: We do not request, access, or track your GPS location, phonebook contacts, or call logs.

2. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

MicrobusinessFlow only requests permissions strictly necessary to perform its advertised core functions in full compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies:

A. Microphone Access (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO)

  • Purpose: Used solely to enable the Hands-Free Voice Expense Entry feature, allowing you to dictate expense amounts and categories.
  • Processing: Speech input is converted to text either entirely on-device via the Android system's speech recognition engine or through standard system speech recognition APIs.
  • Zero Retention: We do not record, store, transmit, or analyze your raw audio files. Audio data is discarded immediately after text extraction.

B. Biometric Hardware Access (android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT)

  • Purpose: Powers the optional Biometric App Lock, protecting your bookkeeping data from unauthorized physical access to your device.
  • Security: Authentication is handled strictly by Android’s hardware-backed BiometricPrompt and Trusted Execution Environment (TEE). The app receives only a binary result (success or failure) and never has access to your fingerprint or facial biometric templates.

C. Storage & File Sharing (FileProvider / Storage Access Framework)

  • Purpose: Used when you explicitly choose to export your expenses into a CSV spreadsheet file for tax preparation, accounting, or backup.
  • Security: The app utilizes Android's secure FileProvider. Exported files are written only when requested and shared exclusively with the application or storage destination you manually designate.

D. Internet & Network State (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)

  • Purpose: Required exclusively to load non-intrusive banner advertisements provided by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and verify network availability.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app integrates third-party software development kits (SDKs) to provide advertisement support:

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

We use Google Mobile Ads to deliver banner ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect certain diagnostic and device data, including:

  • IP address (for coarse geographic ad delivery and fraud prevention)
  • Mobile Advertising Identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID), subject to device-level opt-out settings
  • Application performance metrics and interaction logs for ad impressions

This data collection is governed directly by Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more about how Google manages advertising data by reviewing Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all bookkeeping and expense records are stored locally on your device:

  • You retain complete, sole ownership and control over your data at all times.
  • You can delete individual transactions or categories directly within the app interface.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing the app’s storage cache from your Android Settings permanently and irrevocably deletes all your stored financial entries from the device.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

MicrobusinessFlow is a financial management tool intended exclusively for business owners, freelancers, and adult professionals. The application is not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security Measures

We are dedicated to safeguarding your business data. MicrobusinessFlow enforces security best practices by keeping all business records inside the sandboxed Android application private storage directory, enforcing edge-to-edge hardware biometric safeguards, and eliminating external cloud vulnerability points by maintaining an offline-first architecture.

7. GDPR & CCPA Rights

Under regulations including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right of Access & Portability: You can export all your financial records at any time using the built-in One-Tap CSV Export feature.
  • Right to Erasure: You can delete all your stored data instantly by clearing the app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
  • No Sale of Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial information to third parties.
